Microphone

Put the phone or laptop a couple of metres from the kit. Headphones give the cleanest results; with speakers, run the speaker test below. Raise the sensitivity until your ghost notes register; lower it if hits appear when you're not playing.

Latency calibration

Every device has a delay between a sound being scheduled and the mic hearing it. Calibrate once per device (and again if you switch speakers or headphones).

Focus

Tap the waveform to move the loop to a bar line; drag an edge to change it (Free). Slowing down keeps the pitch; drum hits stay crisp. Kick/Snare/Hats filter the sound so one part is easier to hear.

Rough transcription

Finds kick, snare and hi-hat hits in the loop (up to 4 bars from the loop start, which should be a beat 1) and puts them on a grid. It's a starting point: check it by ear and fix it in the Builder. Cross-sticks and claps show as snare; rides and shakers as hats.
